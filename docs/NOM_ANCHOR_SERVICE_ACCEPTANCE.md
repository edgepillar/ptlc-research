# NoM anchor service acceptance obligations

Baseline:
[`29a83ac07c27e9d66c12954feffc4c9946318725`](https://github.com/edgepillar/ptlc-research/tree/29a83ac07c27e9d66c12954feffc4c9946318725).
This requirements document connects the selected local retention evidence to the
unfilled independent service gates. It selects no operational authority, storage
product, transport, clock, signature message, deployment or recovery mechanism.
It adds no executable protocol or permission. Application/core remain **NO-GO**,
publication outcomes remain **UNKNOWN**, and recovery action remains **NONE**.

## What the accepted baseline establishes

The [transition journal](NOM_ANCHOR_TRANSITION_JOURNAL.md) retains one exact public
request and its optional functional transition. Explicit exclusive creation and
existing-file open are separate. Reopen requires complete actual original custody;
public descriptors, digests and recovered bytes cannot replace that custody.
Selected writes and complete reopen return only local synchronization evidence.
The file admits two canonical records for one fixed request; it cannot act as a
growing service ledger.

The baseline qualifier has eight relation controls, twelve refusals and four
successful unsafe controls at 228 measured inputs. Its 24 directed methods include
selected actual process-death cuts and a native file-size refusal. These are
existing observations, not additional executions caused by this document. Their
[validation scope](NOM_ANCHOR_TRANSITION_JOURNAL_VALIDATION.md) supplies no physical
power-loss, independent-machine or operational service acceptance.

The [lifecycle](NOM_ANCHOR_LIFECYCLE.md) retains exact possible publications and
terminal reference tombstones. The [read-order model](NOM_CHECKPOINT_READ_ORDERING.md)
retains an evaluation position. The [response-signature profile](NOM_CHECKPOINT_RESPONSE.md)
qualifies separate public signature mathematics. None authenticates an operational
anchor lifecycle or independently provisions a current service.

## Required service boundary

Service selection must precede an incoming service reply. The selected authority,
realm, service identity, epoch, policy, work scope and initial retention obligation
must have independently retained provenance. Selecting a new key encoding,
directory, process, replica or epoch is not evidence of a new allowance or safe
absence. Distinct encodings do not establish independent custody domains.

The selected domain must preserve the complete original-operation commitments,
charged UNKNOWN work, possible disclosures, pending exact requests and terminal
history. Required actual custody must remain available separately. This document
defines no transport serialization for the reference model's typed Python objects.
An authenticated digest cannot reconstruct an absent original or evidence opening.

Source truth, canonicality, finality, freshness policy and account/contract
semantics remain independent obligations. An anchor service cannot grant a
trusted-chain verdict merely by retaining or signing a supplied checkpoint.

## Gates before operational adoption

Every gate below is **UNFILLED**. Its required evidence is a condition for a future
assessed construction, not a claim about the selected local file or CI results.

| Gate | Required evidence | Existing boundary that must remain visible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AS-1: independent provisioning | An independently selected authority and complete realm/service/epoch/work-scope binding, including provenance, revocation and transition rules outside coherent consumer restore. | A packet-selected root, fresh key encoding or fresh challenge supplies no provisioning. |
| AS-2: enrollment and retirement continuity | One retained enrollment obligation for the selected scope, explicit creation authority, and terminal history that survives relocation, alias selection and epoch/key changes. | A missing file, outage or new directory cannot establish safe absence or erase a tombstone. |
| AS-3: shared durable transitions | A shared expected-state comparison that commits the exact request, complete retained obligations and resulting head before an operational acknowledgment. Partial failure and lost replies must retain the original request and both possible outcomes. | Functional transitions and physical-file locks do not serialize independent copies or provide service durability. |
| AS-4: authenticated current evaluation | An independently authorized result bound to the exact query, selected service and complete evaluation opening at a serialized retained position, with assessed message domains and verification roles. | The existing response signatures do not authenticate lifecycle/order roles; a newly signed old head can still be stale. |
| AS-5: external nonrollback retention | An assessed domain outside coherent consumer/source restore, with an explicit fault model and evidence for copy, restore, rollback, replacement and retirement preservation. | Hash chains, local fsync, matching old inputs and independent filenames do not establish this domain. |
| AS-6: original-operation entry | A separately reviewed atomic boundary between retained work authorization and the exact original operation, preserving its existing charge across every ambiguous outcome. Source/chain acceptance must also remain independently qualified. | No lookup, signature result, new epoch or successful reopen authorizes another original, retry, replacement or replenishment. |
| AS-7: independent qualification | Review of the complete selected construction and original evidence, independent-machine/system fault qualification, and a declared physical durability acceptance scope. | Local regression, hosted CI and selected process death do not discharge independent assessment or hardware power-loss requirements. |

A construction may propose different mechanisms for these obligations. It must
name which failure domains remain trusted and which guarantees are conditional.
Passing one gate does not imply another. A proposal or checklist is not a passed
gate, and a storage technology name is not a qualification result.

Capacity exhaustion must preserve the last complete obligation and refuse further
extension without dropping charges or terminal history. Migration and epoch/key
changes need explicit continuity evidence; they cannot reset a finite bound by
implicitly bootstrapping another service identity. No history-compaction or
garbage-collection protocol is selected here.

## Exact lookup and acknowledgment contract to assess

A future current lookup must start from the independently selected service
expectation and retained known minimum. It must not learn its trusted root,
identity, epoch, policy or expected scope from the incoming reply. Its review
packet must account for all of the following semantic bindings:

- The complete selected authority, service, realm, epoch, policy and work scope.
- The exact query/challenge and exact retained request being looked up, including
  its complete before-state expectation and proposed transition commitment.
- The evaluated retained head and position, complete evidence opening and lineage
  from the retained minimum, including all UNKNOWN charges and terminal history.
- The service outcome at that evaluation position and its durability/retention
  meaning, with explicit handling of outage, missing custody and interrupted work.
- The precise verification role and message/domain construction, independently
  assessed against the existing source, custody and response signature roles.

These are requirements, not a new wire schema or signed message. No existing
domain, public fixture signature or callback permission is extended to cover them.
Counts, public descriptors and boolean verifier results cannot replace the complete
bindings. The service's revision, checkpoint sequence, ordered-event position and
original-operation charge remain distinct quantities.

An authenticated lookup can describe only the selected evaluation instant under
its assessed service assumptions. A later publication can make that result old at
delivery. The construction must state its ordering and delivery limits instead
of treating a fresh challenge, larger counter or signature as delivery currentness.

A request not found at one evaluated position does not prove global absence,
unused work, loss of an earlier enrollment or permission to repeat an original.
A matching retained request does not settle the original operation's outcome.
An outage supplies no candidate head or cached positive substitute. Larger local
or independently retained UNKNOWN work must remain charged in every case.

## Reconciliation obligations exposed by existing files

| Selected local observation | Obligation before any operational interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Existing file missing | Preserve the exact original enrollment/request obligation and require independently selected reconciliation. Do not create a replacement or infer absence. |
| Complete first record only | Retain the exact pending request and proposed UNKNOWN work. Local absence of the second record resolves no possible service publication. |
| Complete second record after a lost acknowledgment | Retain the complete image and original custody. A new local fsync acknowledgment supplies no earlier service acknowledgment or current authenticated evaluation. |
| Complete retirement record | Preserve the full terminal history. Its presence is a reference observation; global retirement continuity still requires AS-2 and AS-5. |
| Torn, reordered, altered or extra bytes | Preserve and quarantine the selected image without trimming, repair, replacement, retry or automatic reenrollment. |
| Coherent old image or independently writable copies | Treat local agreement as insufficient for unique/current independent retention. Keep every original charge and require the external acceptance gates. |

The comparison must not dispatch an original operation or fill missing actual
custody. The [existing lifecycle comparisons](NOM_ANCHOR_LIFECYCLE.md#lost-acknowledgment-and-exact-lookup)
already retain both publication possibilities without recovery authority. A
future service receipt needs its own assessed meaning before changing that boundary.

## Counterexamples retained as acceptance inputs

The following are the exact successful unsafe control identifiers in the pinned
[qualifier](https://github.com/edgepillar/ptlc-research/blob/29a83ac07c27e9d66c12954feffc4c9946318725/scripts/qualify_nom_anchor_transition_journal.py).
They remain counterexamples to the selected construction, not expected failures
of an implemented independent service.

| Existing control | Missing independent property | Evidence required from a candidate construction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `coherent_old_restore` | A matching old file and matching old actual inputs can reopen. | Preserve the selected newer independent obligation when every consumer-side copy is old. |
| `independent_file_copies` | Two different physical files independently append the fixed transition. | Demonstrate one shared retained authority for the selected scope and exact expected-state decisions across all participating copies. |
| `explicit_reenrollment` | Explicit creation in another directory selects the old request again. | Demonstrate that local creation, alias choice and epoch/key transitions cannot silently mint another enrollment or erase old charged work. |
| `restored_pre_retirement` | An earlier complete image hides a later retirement transition. | Preserve and authenticate terminal history through the declared restore, migration and fault model. |

The existing selected death cuts, torn-file refusals and real file-size limit are
also required review inputs. Replaying those same local experiments against a new
pathname is not independent-domain evidence. Any future fault campaign must pin
the exact implementation, runtime/platform, original inputs, output completeness
and actual exit, and preserve failed or interrupted evidence separately.

## Next implementation decision

Before implementing an operational service adapter, prepare one candidate design
that identifies its independently retained authority, provisioning/lifecycle
protocol, fault model, shared transition boundary and exact evaluation semantics.
Map its mechanisms and limitations to AS-1 through AS-7, including counterexamples
it cannot prevent. Select and assess any new signature roles and framing explicitly.
No such candidate is selected or accepted by this document.

An offline reference implementation may exercise a proposed interface with
synthetic public inputs while keeping every gate unfilled. It must label its
assumptions and preserve the distinction between a modeled external authority and
an actually independent one. A simulated rejection is not nonrollback proof.

Keep this service design, session recovery and chain observation outside the core
node contribution. Bitcoin transaction construction remains separate as well.
Deployment, node activation, wallet access, application signing, transaction
broadcast and real funds remain outside the authorized offline workspace.
No automatic retry, same-request republication, replenishment, replacement,
bootstrap or reenrollment permission is introduced.
