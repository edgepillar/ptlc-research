## Directed controls

Controls cover both complete synthetic originals, both full funding hash-input
records, exact raw momentum bytes and all eight explicitly unselected profiles.
They separate commitment equality, advertised/computed hash equality, exact
Create fields, send-derived entry binding and recomputed header membership.
Wrong amount/token/funder/contract, decoded expiry/point/key/fixed recipient,
non-Create or aliased ABI, changed shaped original and false advertised hashes
retain their own comparison results. All supported/contradicted labels remain
claims, including a supported payload that locally differs from the selection.

The frozen old entry ids differ from matching recomputed Create hashes on both
legs. A separate unsigned computed-id/header selection with shaped zero inner
signatures passes all selected comparisons yet has no account authorization,
live entry, actual signed wire or source authority. Invalid acknowledged momentum
or unqualified Plasma fields can coexist with matching Create fields; no account
admission verdict is inferred. Missing momentum means membership is unchecked.

Exact claim-wire and domain-separated payload commitments refuse status
relabeling, changed payloads, raw whitespace/key/signature substitution and
cross-query challenge/network/checkpoint/original/consumer selections. Tests
cover partial/unknown/unavailable openings, unsupported proof profiles, missing
legs, swapped originals, malformed terms, immutable returned copies, exact replay
and fresh challenges over old claims. They require retention/no-replacement flags,
funding/live-entry revalidation and action `NONE` under every admitted result.

Bounds cover outer bytes/depth before decoding, bounded hex before allocation,
two complete originals, momentum bytes and funding payload total size, alongside
unchanged account field/number/data/descendant rules. Duplicate/escaped keys,
nonfinite/non-ASCII bytes, alternate canonical encodings, extra fields and wrong
input/status types refuse with sanitized diagnostics. A direct no-file/process
I/O control distinguishes pure comparisons from persistent retention or dispatch.
These tests supply no replacement-protocol security or independent proof review.
