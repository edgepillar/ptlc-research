projection, pure comparison helper and fresh-source qualifier. It preserves the
complete earlier VM/source, four-role opening and recovery subjects. It adds no
authority fact and does not select an application observer or recovery admission.

The directed suite has 36 controls. Positive cases require all ten projections
and preserve failed Unlock/Reclaim and raw replay counterexamples. Adversarial

heights, absent required roles, advertised-hash substitution, opaque patch-hash
changes, acknowledgement alias/regression, orphan/out-of-window children,
wrong network/account/type, empty/null windows and independently changed anchors.

selections, refuse uncommitted changes and unknown with a present payload, and
enforce record/query/wire/depth limits and strict canonical framing. Mocked native
failure verifies failed-evidence retention only; it is not a source pass.

and cannot substitute for them. The preserved overlay paths require `-vet=off`
